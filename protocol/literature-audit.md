# Literature screening, 7 October 2026

This initial targeted search is not a systematic review or proof of absence. Full-text and implementation access are still being checked.

| Source | Evidence inspected | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Budget-aware two-stage NetFlow intrusion detection for high-traffic critical information infrastructure under domain shift (2026), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S187454822600048X | Publisher-indexed abstract and sections describe RiskGate-IDS, three NetFlow-v3 datasets, target calibration and budgeted inspection. Direct full-text retrieval unsuccessful; advertised GitHub repository returned 404. | Broad budgeted cross-domain IDS is already covered. Do not present our broad question as novel or call an approximation a reproduced RiskGate baseline. |
| Selective Cost-Aware Random Forests for Unreliable Data, https://openreview.net/pdf?id=McQ73Hv4Ru | Indexed abstract covers public credit data, missingness, covariate shift and abstention. Full text retrieval pending. | Missingness plus selective prediction is not itself new. Need task-specific evidence beyond application renaming. |
| DEA-IDS: Drift-Aware Feature Selection and Few-Shot Adaptation for Cross-Domain IoT-IoMT Intrusion Detection (2026), https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/19/6200 | Publisher sections include source/target separation and operational false-positive evaluation. | Compare source-only versus target-adapted information regimes fairly; false-positive and rare-family behavior matter. |
| Temporal Analysis of NetFlow Datasets for Network Intrusion Detection Systems, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04404v3 | Author abstract/version record, revised 28 August 2026, related DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3688204. | Prefer temporal v3 audit; never infer temporal ordering from v2 row order. |
| Selective Question Answering under Domain Shift (2020), https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.503/ | Author abstract and methodology excerpts. | Learned error scoring and confidence failure under domain shift are established outside IDS. |
| MaskAug: Architecture-agnostic corruption-aware training for robust network intrusion detection (14 September 2026), https://doi.org/10.1177/0926227X261487917 | Publisher abstract reports random masking, block masking and proportional noise injection; restricted full text. | Feature-mask augmentation for IDS is already published. Treat it as an established baseline family, not our new method. |
| Gradient-guided stress adaptation for streaming intrusion detection under degraded telemetry (2026), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417426016556 | Publisher-indexed abstract describes uncertainty-triggered stress synthesis and degraded telemetry. Full text and exact publication date unverified. | Streaming uncertainty and corruption adaptation are also overlapping research. |
| Intrusion Detection under Incomplete Information (2026), https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202609.0001 | Author preprint abstract/conclusion. Not peer-reviewed. | Incomplete information is a broad decision-support framing, not a novel algorithm category. |

Candidate gap: a controlled joint-shift evaluation contrasting source-calibrated review thresholds with hard review caps under structured flow-feature outages. This remains provisional until closest full texts and missing-feature IDS literature are assessed. A negative result or failed novelty gate is acceptable.
